![Experience Progress](https://storage.googleapis.com/rnda-mod-banners/experienceprogress-title-round.png)

Show current experience level and amount needed to level up as numbers over the XP bar. Toggle visibility with F3 + X. Works similar to the vanilla render hitboxes feature.

![](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/WLzTG5bH/images/13af5b6d756c68557e6e63de2f4700a2944b51dc.png)

Made for the Minecraft streamer [Linkzzey](https://twitch.tv/linkzzey) over on Twitch!
